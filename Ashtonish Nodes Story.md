🚀 The Creative Journey
01. The Spark ✨
Every piece starts as a fragile idea — a phrase, a mood, a "what if."
•	Super Prompt Workshop: Where your ideas take shape. Build from your personal prompt library, drop in wildcards, and watch one idea bloom into a hundred directions.
•	Dynamic Prompt Engine: Turns structured ideas into living variations.
•	Super LLM Node: A local collaborator right beside you — your machine, your model, your rules — brainstorming prompts and aspect ratios on demand.

02. The Deep Dive 🌊
Now you're exploring with full momentum.
•	The Sequencer: Walks through your prompt variations one by one, making each generation deliberate.
•	Aspect Orchestrator: Dynamic canvas management — portrait for the hero shot, wide for the landscape — operating automatically in rhythm with your batch.
•	Batch Processor: Feeds your image directory through the workflow while you step back and let the studio run itself.

❌ Where most stories end: A hard drive full of brilliance and a creator who can't remember which spark made which fire.

✨ Yours doesn't end there.
03. The Capture 📸
Every image you save through ASHTONISH carries its full story — the prompt, the seed, the settings, the model — written invisibly into the file.
The moment an image is generated, the Prompt Vault opens its pages:
•	📜 Full Prompt & Seed
•	🖼️ Thumbnail
•	🎛️ Sampler & Model
•	📐 Dimensions & Generation Time

All recorded automatically, without you lifting a finger. You will never again generate something extraordinary and lose the recipe for it. That's not a feature. That's the whole point.

04. The Memory 🧠
Weeks later — months later — you type two words into the Vault and there it is.
•	Universal Search: Find anything by a tag, the model used, the date, dimension, or client name.
•	Instant Favorites: Your best work rises immediately to the top.
•	Sister Image Linking: Wildcards explored together stay linked as sister images — the entire family of one experiment, browsable side-by-side with the flip of a chevron.
Your best work stops being something you had and becomes something you own.

05. The Gallery 📁
Because this is a working studio, not a shoebox:
•	Organize outputs into dedicated Projects and Clients (e.g., The brand campaign for Skyscreen, Personal experiments, "Someday" ideas).
•	Rate your work while it's fresh. Star what sings.
•	Archive finished work — never deleted, just resting, one click from returning.

06. The Return 🔄
And when you find that gem from three months ago?
One click, and the prompt is back in your workflow. Same seed, same settings — remix it, iterate on it, create the next evolution. Your past work becomes your creative leverage. The you of three months ago becomes a collaborator you can always reach.
